▸ Architecture
data_pipeline/

DataIngester — yfinance fetching with exponential backoff and Parquet caching for reproducible runs.

features/

FeatureEngineer — log returns, SMA, EMA, RSI, ATR. All signals lagged by one bar to prevent look-ahead bias.

strategies/

Abstract BaseStrategy + concrete implementations: EMA crossover baseline and Random Forest ML strategy.

backtester/

Event-driven HistoricalBacktester — strictly chronological iteration, position sizing, and fee accounting.

performance/

RiskAnalytics — Sharpe ratio, annualised volatility, max drawdown, win/loss ratio from equity curve.

tests/

Unit tests on deterministic synthetic data — no network access required. Runs in CI on every push.
